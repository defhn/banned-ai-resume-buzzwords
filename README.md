## ⚡ Why Recruiters Are Rejecting AI-Generated Resumes

In 2024–2026, millions of job seekers fed raw job descriptions into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini with prompts like:
> *"Rewrite my resume bullet points to sound executive, impactful, and professional."*

The result? Recruiters now review hundreds of resumes per job post that all sound identical:
* Every candidate *"spearheaded cross-functional paradigms"*.
* Every junior engineer *"orchestrated mission-critical microservices"*.
* Every customer support agent created *"a testament to operational excellence"*.

When hiring managers spot these repetitive AI markers, they don't just suspect LLM usage — **they assume you are bluffing**. When they probe these bullet points during the 15-minute initial phone screen, candidates stumble because they cannot articulate the granular mechanics of what they claimed to "spearhead."

## 🎯 The 15-Minute Phone Screen Trap

Recruiters don't run AI detectors to disqualify you. They use **behavioral probing questions** to see if your resume was written by an LLM:

### Example: The "Spearhead" Trap
* **Resume Bullet**: *"Spearheaded the organizational transition to automated testing paradigms across all departments."*
* **Recruiter Question**: *"Walk me through the resistance you met from team leads when introducing this, the specific CI runner config you chose, and what failed during week one?"*
* **The Failure**: If ChatGPT wrote this bullet, you will freeze or offer generic theory.
* **Defensible Replacement**: *"Wrote 14 integration test suites in Vitest for the auth service; reduced regression bugs caught in staging by 35% over 6 months."*
